# Large Business Contracts Bucket - Status and Locations

Compiled on November 14, 2026. This "bucket" aggregates publicly available information on businesses handling major (primarily federal/government) contracts, their current statuses, and primary locations/HQ or places of performance.

**Sources**: GovCon Wire, GovBidWatch, USAspending.gov via aggregators (FederalBidAlerts, ObligationWatch, GovHub), SAM.gov data. Data is approximate and subject to updates; always verify on official sites.

## Overview of Big Contracts Landscape
- Federal agencies obligated ~$796.9 billion in contracts in the last 12 months (to Nov 9, 2026).
- Top 10 contractors took 29%, top 100 took 60%, small businesses 21%.
- Dominant agencies: DoD (~54%), VA, DHS, DOE.
- Top states by awards: Virginia, California, Texas, Florida, New York, Maryland.
- Many contracts are IDIQs (multiple award), with ongoing task orders; statuses are typically "awarded/active," with some nearing end dates (options often exercised).

## Contract Statuses and End Dates
- Majority of big contracts are multi-year active, with options.
- ObligationWatch tracks 11,997 large contracts (> $10M) with end dates in next 18 months totaling $1.17T committed. Many end soon but often extended.
- Examples of near-term (as of Nov 13-14, 2026): Various sustainment, IT, construction for Army, Navy, Air Force, VA – locations like VA, MD, TX, NC, FL.
- Status: Active until end date; recompetes possible but not guaranteed.

## Locations ("Where They Are Sitting")
- **HQ concentrations**: Virginia (NoVA / Arlington / Reston / Falls Church) dominates for defense/IT contractors due to proximity to DoD/Pentagon. California (tech/space), Texas (energy/construction), Florida, Maryland, Massachusetts, Colorado.
- **Place of Performance**: Nationwide, but heavy in VA, CA, TX, LA, WA, NY. Construction often site-specific (bases, borders, stadiums).
- Top states by recent awards: VA, CA, TX, FL, NY, MD.

## Key Aspects Tracked
- **Value**: Obligations vs. ceilings (ceilings much higher for IDIQs).
- **Type**: Defense (aircraft, missiles, ships), IT/modernization, construction, healthcare (VA/DoD), logistics, energy.
- **Set-asides**: ~52% small business opportunities in recent postings.
- **Trends**: Heavy DoD spend, cloud/IT modernization, infrastructure, space, border-related.
